# Status Pill

A compact label for machine state: running, failed, offline. Uppercase-adjacent
tracking and a pill radius keep it distinct from a button.

## Variations

Five states ship: `idle`, `active`, `warning`, `danger` and `offline`. Each only
reassigns colour, background and border.

<Canvas of={PillStories.Example} />

> The block is `kd-status-pill` but its custom properties are named `--kd-pill-*`. Both
> names are public API and cannot be renamed without a breaking change.

## With a dot

`kd-status-pill__dot` adds a leading dot that inherits the pill's colour through
`currentcolor`, so it needs no variation of its own. On the `active` variation the dot
pulses.

<Canvas of={PillStories.WithDot} />

> The pulse is an infinite animation. It is covered by the reduced-motion guard in the
> reset entry. If you load `./css/components` without `./css/reset`, add your own
> `prefers-reduced-motion` rule.

## Customising

`--kd-pill-color`, `--kd-pill-bg`, `--kd-pill-border` and `--kd-pill-dot-size` are
declared on the block. Reassign them on the element for a one-off state rather than
adding a variation.
