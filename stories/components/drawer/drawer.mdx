# Drawer

The mobile navigation overlay: a scrim covering the page and a panel slid in from the
inline end. It is two elements, and the block itself is the scrim.

## Example

The drawer is `position: fixed` with `inset: 0`, so in a real page it covers the viewport
and blurs whatever sits behind it. Every demo here is boxed and switched to `absolute` so
it stays inside the docs page.

<Canvas of={DrawerStories.Example} />

## The panel is the menu

`kd-drawer__panel` is the menu `<ul>` itself, not a wrapper around one. That is the whole
contract, and it is what makes the drawer free: the items you already wrote for the navbar
can be cloned straight in, and `kd-navbar__link` and `kd-btn` keep styling themselves with
no drawer-specific rules.

<Canvas of={DrawerStories.Menu} />

## Closing

Opening runs two animations at once, `kd-fade-in` on the scrim and `kd-slide-in-end` on
the panel. Adding `kd-drawer--closing` swaps both names for their mirrors, `kd-fade-out`
and `kd-slide-out-end`, so the close is the same motion reversed rather than a second set
of rules. The demo below loops the pair so it stays on screen; in a real drawer it plays
once and holds.

<Canvas of={DrawerStories.Closing} />

> Both animations are `forwards` and neither one removes anything. Add `--closing`, wait
> for `animationend`, then unmount. Unmounting on the click instead means the closing
> animation is never seen.

## Customising

`--kd-drawer-width`, `--kd-drawer-bg`, `--kd-drawer-scrim` and `--kd-drawer-blur` are
declared on the block. Width takes any length, so `100%` gives a full-bleed sheet, and
`--kd-drawer-blur: 0px` leaves the scrim as a flat wash with nothing behind it softened.
