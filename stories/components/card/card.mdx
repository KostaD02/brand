# Card

A bordered surface for grouping related content. The card is a flex column, so its
children stack and the footer can be pinned to the bottom. It is also
`position: relative`, so an absolutely positioned child resolves against the card.

## Example

`kd-card__body` holds the content, `kd-card__title` and `kd-card__text` set the type.
None of the elements are required; a card with a single child is valid.

<Canvas of={CardStories.Example} />

## Surface

`kd-card--surface` drops the background from `--kd-bg-elevated` to `--kd-bg-surface`.
Use it when a card sits on top of an already elevated area and needs to read as recessed
rather than raised.

<Canvas of={CardStories.Surface} />

## Header

`kd-card__header` is the third slot alongside `__body` and `__footer`: a full width row
with the label on the left and whatever acts on it on the right. It aligns its children
to the top, so a two line title does not drag the pill or the icon link down with it.

<Canvas of={CardStories.Header} />

## Footer

`kd-card__footer` has `margin-top: auto`, so it is pushed to the bottom of the card. In a
row of cards with unequal text, the footers still line up.

<Canvas of={CardStories.Footer} />

## Media

`kd-card__media` cancels the card padding with a negative margin and rounds its top
corners to match, so an image meets the border with no gap.

<Canvas of={CardStories.Media} />

> Media only clips correctly as the first child. Placing it mid-card leaves the rounded
> corners pointing the wrong way.

## Interactive

`kd-card--interactive` applies the pop treatment: the card lifts and casts an offset
shadow on hover and focus. Use it only when the whole card is a single link or button.

<Canvas of={CardStories.Interactive} />

## Lift

`kd-card--lift` raises the card straight up and puts a soft shadow under it, where
`kd-card--interactive` moves it diagonally onto a hard offset shadow. Reach for `--lift`
in a grid of project cards, where several cards move under the same pointer sweep and a
diagonal shift reads as noise; reach for `--interactive` on a single card that is the
call to action.

<Canvas of={CardStories.Lift} />

> Neither variation sets `outline: none`, unlike the button. A keyboard focused card
> shows the dashed focus ring on top of the movement, which is the behaviour you want.

## Title colour

`--kd-card-title-color` and `--kd-card-title-hover-color` drive `kd-card__title`, and the
title carries the shared transition so it fades rather than snaps. Both
`kd-card--interactive` and `kd-card--lift` move the title to the hover colour on `:hover`,
`:focus-visible` and `:focus-within`, so the surface and its heading change together and
the card reads as one target rather than a box with a link somewhere inside it.
`:focus-within` is the case that matters when the card is a plain element wrapping a link:
tabbing to the link still tints the title. Both properties are declared on the block, so
one card can take a different hover colour without a new modifier.

<Canvas of={CardStories.TitleColor} />

## Covering the card

`kd-card` sets `position: relative`, which makes it the containing block for an absolutely
positioned descendant. That is what the `kd-stretch` utility on
[Position](?path=/docs/utilities-position--docs) needs: put `kd-stretch` on the title link
and its `::before` fills the card, so the whole surface follows that one link. Anchors are
relative themselves for the underline sweep, so add `kd-pos-static` to the link; anything
that has to stay separately clickable, such as the icon link in the footer, takes
`kd-z-raised`.

<Canvas of={CardStories.Stretched} />

> Without `position: relative` on the card the overlay would resolve against whichever
> ancestor happened to be positioned. In a grid of cards that is usually the grid itself,
> so every card's overlay covers the whole grid, the overlays pile up on each other, and
> only the last card in the DOM stays clickable.

## Customising

`--kd-card-padding`, `--kd-card-bg`, `--kd-card-border-color`, `--kd-card-radius`,
`--kd-card-title-color` and `--kd-card-title-hover-color` are declared on the block, so
reassigning any of them on the element restyles that one card. `kd-card--flush` is the
built-in shortcut for zero padding.
